About diamonds

Are lab-grown diamonds real?

Yes! Lab-grown diamonds are 100% real diamonds. They have the same chemical, physical and optical properties as natural diamonds.

Which is better — natural or lab-grown?

It depends on your preference! Natural diamonds are for rarity and legacy. Lab-grown diamonds are for value, sustainability and modern luxury. Both are excellent choices.

What is the price difference?

Lab-grown diamonds cost 30–50% less than natural diamonds of the same size and quality.

Do you offer natural and lab-grown diamonds?

Yes. We specialise in bespoke diamond jewellery using both natural and lab-grown diamonds, tailored to your style, values and budget. Share your preference with us and we will talk you through the options for your design.

How do I know the quality of a diamond?

We follow the 4Cs: Cut, Colour, Clarity and Carat Weight. Each diamond is graded and certified. You can also explore our Diamond Guide to understand the grades.

Do you provide certification?

Yes. Our jewellery is certified by IGI (International Gemological Institute), IMA GEM, IDI and IDT, ensuring trust, transparency and accurate grading. Ask us for the certificate details of your selected diamond.

Is diamond jewellery a good investment?

Diamonds are timeless pieces with lasting emotional value. Natural and lab-grown diamonds are both chosen for their beauty and meaning, while resale value depends on the diamond and the market. Ask us about our buy-back terms for your jewellery.

Orders & policies

Is it safe to get jewellery customised instead of buying online?

Yes, and it's even safer. When you customise with us, you get personal consultation, verified diamonds, certification, expert craftsmanship and after-sales support, all with complete transparency.

What are the payment and delivery terms?

The stated order terms are 50% advance, the gold rate is fixed at booking, and the remaining 50% is payable on delivery. The stated delivery timeline is 21 working days. Custom orders are non-cancellable.

Do you provide shipping and a buy-back policy?

Yes. We offer free shipping and a 100% transparent buy-back option for your diamond jewellery, subject to our terms and conditions. It gives you the freedom and flexibility for the future. Contact us for the buy-back details for your piece.
